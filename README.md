## Structure

- `services.html` — Interactive odd-job checklist synced to the quote form
- `areas.html` — Eaglescliffe (`TS16`), Yarm (`TS15`) & surrounding village coverage
- `quote.html` — Accessible client-side demo quote enquiry form (transmits nothing externally)

## Set D — Send a Photo, Get a Price

- `d/index.html`, `d/css/styles.css`, `d/js/app.js` — The whole site is one action: snap the job, say where you are, get a price back. Van-livery yellow and black.
- Live at https://tedotedo.github.io/ey-handyman-demo/d/. Reuses the root `fonts/` and `images/`; same `noindex`, zero external requests and `.ph` placeholder rules as Sets A–C.
- The availability week is illustrative. The "before" picture is the same illustrative photo greyed out, labelled on the page. Photos chosen in the demo stay on the phone; nothing is uploaded.
